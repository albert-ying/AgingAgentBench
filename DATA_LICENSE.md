# Data reuse

The ClockEffectBench data compilation and ClockBase numerical outputs are
licensed under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International license
[CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).
Attribute ClockEffectBench to Ying K, et al. and cite the ClockBase preprint
DOI 10.1101/2023.02.28.530532. Retain each record's source identifiers when
redistributing or using published contrasts and identify changes you make.

Evidence quotations come from the cited papers. Their copyright remains
with their respective rights holders; the compilation does not relicense
those quotations or the papers themselves.

`data/expert/human_dnam_groups.jsonl` and
`data/expert/human_dnam_samples.jsonl` derive from ComputAgeBench and retain
[CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/).
Attribution, source revision, and modifications are documented in
[the expert annotation notes](data/expert/README.md). This file-specific
license overrides CC BY 4.0 for those derivatives.

`data/expert/mouse_rna_groups.jsonl` contains factual group definitions and
GSM lists derived from Tyshkovskiy et al., Nature 2026, DOI
10.1038/s41586-026-10542-3, Zenodo record 18763485. The record states a license
for clock models and software and none for the annotation. The repository
ships these derived rules and lists with attribution and does not redistribute
the original workbook. The compilation license does not relicense the source
annotation. The separately generated benchmark calls and parsed reference
predictions are included in the CC BY 4.0 compilation.
